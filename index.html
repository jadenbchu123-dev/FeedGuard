Questions

Does this block all AI-generated content?

No — and we won't claim it does. No tool can detect AI content with perfect accuracy. FeedGuard gives you adjustable filters you control: hide Shorts, block channels, filter by keyword, and subscribe to community-reported presets. You decide the strictness.

Will YouTube break this?

YouTube's interface changes occasionally, which can temporarily affect feed-modifying extensions. We keep detection simple and test updates quickly. Extensions like uBlock Origin have operated on YouTube for years — the pattern is well-established.

Does this collect my data?

V0.1 is fully local. No account, no server, no analytics. Your blocklist lives in your browser and never leaves it.

Is this free?

The core extension is free: hide Shorts, block channels, keyword filters. A future paid tier will add community blocklists, device sync, and focus schedules.

Does this work on mobile?

Not yet. V0.1 is a Chrome desktop extension. Mobile support (companion app) is on the roadmap after desktop validation.
